Privacy policy

Train Yard Jam · Last updated 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Train Yard Jam app ("the app") uses and why. The app is made by an independent developer based in India.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (levels cleared, stars, best moves and coins) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

The app downloads a small settings file (chapters.json) from this website to update chapters and events. This request does not include any personal information.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device model and how you interact with ads. You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Levels → Privacy settings in the app.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.

What we do not collect

  • The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
